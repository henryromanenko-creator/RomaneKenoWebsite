Brand Heritage

Roman Keno was born from the benches of custom-made, bespoke jewelry workshops. For years, we poured our passion into crafting finished, high-end pieces for private clientele, learning the intimate language of metal, plating, and stone.

Through this journey, we discovered a gap in the market: independent creators and boutique designers struggled to find reliable, premium-grade components without astronomical markups. We pivoted our expertise. Today, we bridge the gap between European artistry and practical cost-effectiveness.

We source, verify, and curate only the finest materials—ensuring that every jump ring, chain, and clasp meets the rigorous standards of a master jeweler, making luxury accessible to creators worldwide.
